Winnow Lightning — regtest research beta

This update starts from Winnow's wallet and Bitcoin P2P stack. Older app data is preserved separately. Use valueless regtest coins and keep the app open to monitor channels; a seed backup cannot restore channels.

Test funding a channel from Winnow, sending a direct payment, and reconnecting after restart. Then open Settings → Lightning → Send or receive by message. Review the amount plus the 100-sat provider fee, commit, and share the funded claim through the system Share sheet, Copy or a file. Stop the sender, claim from the recipient using a different channel provider, then reconnect the sender to see settlement. A copied claim can be redeemed by anyone holding it; first successful claim wins. Imported text is not a receipt. Expired holds remain pending until channel resolution.

You need a Bitcoin regtest peer and Lightning providers on your LAN. Setup and exact commands:
https://github.com/posix4e/winnow-lightning/blob/codex/winnow-lightning/Lightning/LAB.md

No production Bitcoin or public Lightning-network interoperability is enabled in this beta. BOLT12 refund authentication remains unsupported by the selected PQ core; message payments use the documented private claim format.
